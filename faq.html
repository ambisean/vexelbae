  • Technically speaking, .png files are just another file extension for a type of image. It's very similar to a .jpg or .jpeg file, but are able to carry different properties across. When we ask for a .png file for stickers, it's with implication that you have a contour cut image already pre-made by someone. Since pictures are by default some form of square (rectangles are squares), contour cut images would save as a square meaning it would still print and cut as a square. UNLESS the picture is saved as a .png file -- which will preserve the transparency around the picture. With a .png file (if contour cut) your picture will print and cut as the contoured shape rather than a square.

  • We no longer offer just 1 or 2 custom sticker orders in efforts to keep costs low and save the environment. For this reason, we've changed the quantities to reflect the most economical for both you as the customer and environmental for the planet -- which will utilize as much of the paper as possible while keeping costs low.

  • Colors the machine can print and colors you see on a monitor are different. Computer monitors can display millions of colors as RGB (Red, Green, Blue). The color laser machine uses a 4-color printing process of CMYK (Cyan, Magenta, Yellow, Black). The machine uses these 4 colors to create as many colors as possible, but may not be able to reproduce the colors on your monitor. Additionally, what we see on a computer monitor is emitted light, while what we see on a printed document is reflected light. Due to this difference, we can't get a 100% accurate match. The paper brightness and smoothness will also affect the colors you see printed on the page.

  • Our contour cut stickers usually feature a white border to help the subject stand out from the background. Without a white border around it, the sticker will blend back into the background if you stick it somewhere with similar colors! You can request that your contour cut stickers not have a white border, but it's not recommended if you want to stand out.
